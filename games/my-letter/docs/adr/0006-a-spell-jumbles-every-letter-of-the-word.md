# A Spell jumbles every letter of its word, and a tap never waits

A Spell asked for each capital of its word from three Choices, new ones after every find (ADR 0003). Once the child knew their letters, their grown-up saw them reach for the second letter before the Choices had changed to it, and asked for all the word's letters, scrambled, in place of three. So a Spell now lays out every capital of its word as a Tile, ANNA's two Ns both, in a jumble that is never the word's own order, and the child taps them in turn from left to right. A found Tile goes into its box and leaves its place empty, so the rest don't move under a finger. A Tile tapped out of turn wobbles and is named, as a Fade is, but stays: it's needed later.

The Tiles take a tap as soon as they show, even while the Voice is asking or a found letter is saying its sound, and the tap cuts both off, as in Hop Race. A child who knows the next letter goes straight on. One who doesn't hears the find's sound and then the next ask, as before. Faint letters (on by default) still let a child match each box to its Tile.

We rejected keeping three Choices and only taking the next tap early: the grown-up wanted the whole word in front of the child, and a jumble of every letter is what spelling with letter tiles looks like. We also rejected a switch between the two: three Choices were for a child still learning their letters, and New letters keeps them. The Looks alike rule doesn't apply to Tiles, since they're the word's own letters; a word with B and D shows both.
