# Snack Math

A touch game that teaches a 4-year-old what adding and taking away mean by feeding snacks to animal friends. The child acts out each problem with objects, counts, then picks the numeral.

## Language

### Play

**Friend**:
The animal character the child feeds during a Round (e.g. Bunny, Monkey).
_Avoid_: Character, avatar, pet

**Snack**:
One countable object belonging to a Friend's theme (carrot for Bunny, banana for Monkey).
_Avoid_: Item, token, counter

**Plate**:
The two-rows-of-five grid in front of the Friend where Snacks sit, so amounts are seen as structured groups.
_Avoid_: Ten-frame (in UI copy), tray, board

**Pile**:
The spot beside the Plate holding the Snacks the child must move onto the Plate in an Add Problem.
_Avoid_: Supply, source, inventory

### Problems

**Problem**:
One question: a starting amount, an action, and a result the child names.
_Avoid_: Question, exercise, card

**Add Problem**:
A Problem where more Snacks join the Plate and the child finds the new total.
_Avoid_: Sum, join problem, plus problem

**Take-Away Problem**:
A Problem where the Friend eats some Snacks and the child finds how many are left.
_Avoid_: Subtraction problem, minus problem

**Act-Out**:
The part of a Problem where the child performs the action: dragging Snacks from the Pile, or tapping Snacks for the Friend to eat.
_Avoid_: Manipulation, input phase

**Count-Tap**:
Tapping a Snack on the Plate to count it aloud; each tap says the next number.
_Avoid_: Counting mode, tap-count

**Answer Choices**:
The three large numeral buttons the child picks the result from.
_Avoid_: Options, answers, keypad

**Count-Along**:
The help the game gives after a wrong pick: it counts the Plate aloud with the child, then offers the choices again.
_Avoid_: Hint, correction, retry

### Progress

**Round**:
Five Problems with one Friend, ending in a Sticker. Done once it's finished; there's no failing one.
_Avoid_: Level (the site word; say Round here), session, game

**Stage**:
A difficulty step setting which kinds of Problems appear and how big the numbers get. A Group of four Rounds; any Stage can be picked. Its badge is its sign (+, − or ±) over a Plate of 5 or 10 Snacks. A Group (site term).
_Avoid_: Level, tier, difficulty

**Sticker**:
The reward for finishing a Round; every finished Round earns one, however many mistakes were made.
_Avoid_: Star, badge, points, prize

**Sticker Book**:
Where the child sees every Sticker earned.
_Avoid_: Collection, trophy room

**Grown-up Corner**:
Snack Math's Grown-up Corner (a site term): where a grown-up turns the voice or sounds off, or resets progress.
_Avoid_: Parent mode, admin, settings screen
