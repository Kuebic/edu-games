# Tidy Up

A touch game that teaches two and three-year-olds colors and shapes. The child sorts a Pile of Things into Bins by color, or by shape.

## Language

### Play

**Round**:
One Pile sorted into its Bins, then Tidy. A Round sorts by color or by shape, never both.
_Avoid_: Level, game, turn

**Bin**:
Where Things go: a bucket or a dump truck painted its color on Colors, or a wooden shape-sorter Box cut with its shape on Shapes. Two or three stand along the bottom, as many as the Scope has items, up to three.
_Avoid_: Box (only the shape-sorter), basket, target, bucket (only the Skin)

**Pile**:
The Round's Things, heaped small at the top, one fewer each time one hops off. Four for each of two Bins, three for each of three, and never three in a row for one Bin. It shows how much is left, so there's no progress bar.
_Avoid_: Queue, deck, stack

**Thing**:
One piece to sort: a block, an everyday thing (an emoji of one color) or a shape, as the Way has it.
_Avoid_: Object, item (a Scope word), piece, card

**Spot**:
Where the next Thing waits, above the middle, until it's dragged.
_Avoid_: Start, slot

**Word**:
The recorded name of a color or shape, said as a Thing lands, after a wrong Bin, and when a Thing is tapped. The Game has no Voice; the Words are clips.
_Avoid_: Voice, label, sound

**Miss**:
A Thing let go over the wrong Bin: a soft bump, it glides back to the Spot, its Word plays and its own Bin glows. On the second Miss it goes into its own Bin by itself. Nothing is counted.
_Avoid_: Mistake, wrong answer, fail

**Nudge**:
After 7 seconds without a touch, the Thing wiggles and its Bin glows; from the second, its Word plays too. Three at most in a row. A Thing never moves by itself when she's idle.
_Avoid_: Hint, idle, timeout

**Tidy**:
The Round's end: the Things bounce, the buckets wobble, the trucks drive off or the boxes rattle, then the site's cheer, a card for the grown-up ("Find something red at home!") and the Next button.
_Avoid_: Cheer (Which Way?'s), reward, win, done

### Practice

Tidy Up is a Practice Game (the site's word): it has no Levels. Practice, Topic, Scope and Range are the site's words, in the site's CONTEXT.md.

**Topics**:
Colors (red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink; the Range 🔴🔵🟡 and All) and Shapes (circle, square, triangle, star, heart; the Range ⚪🟦🔺 and All). Play waits for two items, since sorting needs two Bins.

**Way**:
What the Things are. On Colors, **Blocks** (one toy block, only its color differs) or **Things** (everyday things). On Shapes, **Plain** (every shape grey) or **Colorful** (each a color to look past). Each holds the other dimensions still first, then varies them.
_Avoid_: Mode, level

**Skin**:
On Colors only: Buckets or Trucks, the Bins. Shapes always use the Box.

**Patterns**:
A Grown-up Corner switch, off to start with. On, each color carries a pattern too, on its blocks, its Bins and a tag on each everyday Thing, for a color-blind child.
_Avoid_: Color-blind mode
