# Numbers stop at 20, and a grown-up picks which way round each Box goes

Supersedes 0002. Counting a Tray of 60 or 100 beans was tedious, so the Numbers Box has two Rounds, 0 to 10 and 11 to 20, and every Tray fits in two rows. That lets a Tray to choose from always be a bar as wide as a row of ten, one under the other, as the first Round already had, so amounts are compared side by side. The Letters Box keeps its five Rounds by fives.

A Round no longer always takes turns both ways round. Seeing beans and finding the numeral, and seeing the numeral and finding the beans, are different associations, and a grown-up may want a child to practise one. So each Box has a Way in the Grown-up Corner: Find the symbol, Find the picture, or Mix, which takes turns as before and is where it starts. It's saved in Find It's own slot of Saved progress, and a reset leaves it, as it's a setting, not progress. It's a setting rather than more Boxes because the child shouldn't have to choose it, and the Box list stays two Tiles.

A save from when Numbers went to 100 keeps its first two Rounds; the rest are ignored.
