# One Game for numbers and letters, on the Math Shelf

Numbers and letters are two Boxes of one Game, not two Games. They play the same way, one thing to look at and three to choose from, and a shared play screen is the whole Game; two folders would copy it, and the Catalog forbids one Game importing another. The site gives a Game one Category, so this one sits on the Math Shelf: its Numbers Box has ten Rounds to the Letters Box's five, and the child picks by the Tile picture, which shows both. Should the site ever let a Game sit on two Shelves, this is the Game that wants it.
