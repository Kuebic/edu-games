# Rounds go by tens for numbers and by fives for letters, and every Find takes a turn each way round

The Numbers Box's Rounds are 0 to 10, then 11 to 20, and so on up to 100, as asked for, so a child who knows the numbers to 10 moves up a decade at a time, and the one unlock rule opens them in that order. The Letters Box's Rounds are five letters each, A to E up to U to Z, so a Round's three Choices can all come from the letters it teaches. Each Round takes turns both ways round, beans then numeral, picture then letter, rather than keeping the two ways in separate Rounds, because recognising a symbol and matching one are the same lesson and a child gets both without a grown-up choosing. Choices are neighbours (one or two away in number, other letters of the Round) so the Find is about telling close things apart, not spotting the odd one out. Beans sit in rows of ten, five and five, so that 47 can be read as four rows and seven and counted aloud by tens after a wrong pick.
