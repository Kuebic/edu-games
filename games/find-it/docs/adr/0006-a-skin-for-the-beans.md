# A Skin for the beans

The user asked for the beans to become jellybeans or other things. Numbers has five Skins, picked from chips on Practice (the site's ADR 0015): beans, jellybeans, ladybugs, stars and strawberries. A Skin changes how a Tray's beans look and what the Voice calls them ("How many strawberries?"), never the Finds, the Tray's rows of ten, five and five, or the Count-along. Beans and jellybeans are drawn in CSS, each jellybean a colour of its own; the rest are emoji, as wide as a bean and square, so a row of ten is as long as ever. There's one Skin for the Game, kept in Find It's slot, since Letters has no beans. We rejected a new counter for every Find, which the user was offered and didn't pick, and Skins in the Grown-up Corner, because a child may want to choose.
