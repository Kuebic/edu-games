# Go Together

A touch game that teaches two to four-year-olds to see which things go together: Connections with pictures. A Board of Cards sits in hidden groups and nobody names them; the child taps the ones that belong together, and a whole group gathers into a Frame and says its Word.

## Language

### Play

**Card**:
One picture on the Board: a big white rounded square with an emoji, one of the site's Pictures. A tap lifts it; another puts it down.
_Avoid_: Tile (a Hub word), Thing (Tidy Up's), piece

**Board**:
One deal of Cards in a grid, 4, 6, 9 or 16, in 2, 2, 3 or 4 Groups, each Group as many Cards as a row is wide, so a row is never one Group. Also the Way chip that picks the size. A Board is this Game's Round.
_Avoid_: Round (in this Game), level, puzzle, grid (in code only)

**Group**:
The Cards that go together: the same picture (Same), one color (Colors), or one kind of thing (Kinds). Nobody names it until it gathers.
_Avoid_: Category (a Shelf word), set, connection, kind (only on Kinds)

**Frame**:
One of the colored empty frames in a row above the grid, as many as the Board has Groups, in Connections' yellow, green, blue and purple, which mean nothing but which is which. A gathered Group hops into the next empty one.
_Avoid_: Bin (Tidy Up's), row, slot, answer

**Lift**:
What a tap does to a Card: it rises, grows and wears a yellow ring. A second tap puts it down.
_Avoid_: select, pick, choose

**Lead**:
The first Card lifted; its Group is the one being collected. When the lead is put down, the earliest Card still up leads.
_Avoid_: anchor, first pick

**Gather**:
A whole Group up: the Cards slide together, a chime, they hop into a Frame and the Word is said once. The child gathers by lifting the last Card; Show Me gathers the rest for her; the last Group left gathers on its first tap.
_Avoid_: solve, match, win, complete

**Miss**:
A tap on a Card outside the lead's Group: a soft bump, it shakes and drops back, and the rest of the lead's Group glows. She can Miss as often as she likes; nothing gathers by itself. Nothing is counted.
_Avoid_: mistake, wrong answer, one away, fail

**Show Me**:
The button under the grid that gathers the lead's Group for her. There only once she has Missed for that lead, gone when the Group gathers or every Card is put down. The one way a Group gathers without her finding it.
_Avoid_: give up, hint, skip, help

**Word**:
A recorded name, said once as a Group gathers: its kind ("animals"), its color ("red") or, on Same, the picture's own name ("dog"). The site's Word (CONTEXT.md). The Game has no Voice and says nothing else.
_Avoid_: label, Voice

**Nudge**:
After 7 seconds without a touch. Nothing up: one Card wiggles. A lead up: the rest of its Group glows, and from the second Nudge its Word plays. Three at most in a row; nothing gathers while she's idle.
_Avoid_: hint, timeout

**Done**:
Every Frame full: the Frames shine, the site's cheer, a card for the grown-up ("Which things at home go together? Find three.") and the Next button, which deals again.
_Avoid_: win, solved

### Practice

Go Together is a Practice Game (the site's word): it has no Levels. Practice, Topic, Scope and Range are the site's words, in the site's CONTEXT.md.

**Topics**:
**Same** (the groups are one picture over again; its Scope the pools the pictures come from: animals, vehicles, food, clothes, toys, plants; one is enough), **Colors** (red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink; the Range 🔴🔵🟡 and All) and **Kinds** (animals, vehicles, food, clothes, toys, plants, birds, fish, fruit, vegetables; the Ranges Easy, the first four, and All). On Colors and Kinds, Play waits for as many items as the Board has Groups.

**Way**:
The Board: ▪▪ 4, ▪▪▪ 6, ▪▪▪ 9 or ▪▪▪▪ 16, the same on every Topic. A new save is on Kinds, Easy, 6.
_Avoid_: Mode, level, size (in code only)
