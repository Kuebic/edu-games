# Judge every tap; there is no submit

Connections lets you pick four words, submit, and learn "one away" or that you were wrong, four mistakes allowed. A two or three-year-old can't hold four picks in mind, can't read a count, and has no idea what a submit button is. So Go Together judges every tap as it lands: the first Card lifted leads, a Card of the lead's group lifts beside it, and a Card from another group is a Miss at once, a bump and a shake, with the lead still up. A Miss tells her nothing more than that, and she tries again, as often as she likes. The last group left gathers on its first tap, because there is nothing to pick it from. Nothing is counted and nothing is lost.

We rejected a submit step with "one away", which is the newspaper's game and a five-year-old's at best; a free pick judged only when the right number was up, which leaves a child who lifted two wrong Cards with no idea which; and treating the first Card tapped as always right, which is what this does, but without the Lead being told by anything on screen but its ring. If the playtest shows she picks freely and wants to be judged at the end, that is the change to make: the picking rule is one pure function (`tap` in `boards.ts`), and the screen only draws its outcomes.

The first build gathered the group by itself on the second Miss, the sorting screen's two-Misses-then-help (site ADR 0016). The first playtest (2026-11-10, a three-and-a-half-year-old) said that was the wrong shape here: the Game made her think, and it finished the group while she was still working out what the group was. On the sorting screen the Bin is named and a Miss is a slip; here the group is the thing to find. So no count of Misses gathers anything. After a Miss a Show Me button appears under the grid, and the group gathers only when she taps it (`showMe` in `boards.ts`). It is not offered before a Miss, so it can't be tapped through a Board without trying. The first build also lit up the rest of the lead's group on a Miss, and on an idle Nudge, and said the group's Word on the second Nudge. All of that went the same day: a glow on the right Cards is the answer, only a tap short. A Nudge now wiggles the lead or Show Me and nothing else. We also built and dropped a spoken clue, the group's Word on a Miss: telling her what a group is, is the grown-up's part, not the Game's.
