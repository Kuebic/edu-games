# No Vehicle starts at the edge of the board

A car parked in the last cell of a Street, facing out, looked like it was already driving off, and one facing in looked like it had just arrived. Neither reads as a car waiting in traffic. So no part of a Vehicle may start in a cell on the board's outer edge. The end of a T-junction stem is not the board's edge, so a Vehicle may still wait there.
