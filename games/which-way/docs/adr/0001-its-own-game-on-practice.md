# Its own Game, on Practice

The first playtest found that a three-year-old loves Traffic Jam but doesn't know what its Arrows mean. The pitch offered teaching that as a Traffic Jam "Chapter 0". We made it a Game of its own instead. Its three Ways (watch, tap Go, pick the Arrow) play nothing like Traffic Jam's clear-the-board puzzle. A new first Chapter would have shifted Traffic Jam's saved Level numbers (Chapter·8 + i), and Traffic Jam's generator makes boards, not single Trips. To keep the link, the car Skin is drawn like a Traffic Jam car and its Arrow in Traffic Jam's white-and-dark style, and the Pick buttons use Robot Path's screen directions (left, up, down, right).

We first proposed Levels in three Groups, Watch then Go then Pick. The user chose Practice (the site's ADR 0014) because arrows are learned by going over them, and a grown-up will switch the Way and the Arrows between sessions, even during play. So the Way (Watch, Go, Pick), the Scope (←, ↑, ↓, →, with the Ranges ←→, ↑↓ and All) and the Skin (puppy, ball, car) are each one tap on the first screen, and there are no Levels, Done marks or reset.
