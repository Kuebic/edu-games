# Which Way?

A touch game that teaches a three-year-old what an arrow means. An Arrow points, and a puppy, ball or car goes that way to its Treat. First she watches, then she sends it, then she picks the Arrow herself. It leads into Traffic Jam's roof Arrows and Robot Path's arrow Commands.

## Language

### Play

**Mover**:
The one thing that goes: a puppy, a ball or a car, as the Skin has it. It sits in the middle and goes to one Spot. The car turns to face its way first; the puppy and the ball just go.
_Avoid_: Character, player, piece

**Spot**:
A place next to the Mover it can go to: left, right, up or down on the screen. Only the Spots whose Arrows are in the Scope show.
_Avoid_: Cell, square, target

**Treat**:
What waits at one Spot: a bone for the puppy, a goal for the ball, a fuel pump for the car. It is always in view before the Mover goes.
_Avoid_: Goal (in site talk), prize, reward

**Arrow**:
Which way on the screen, left, right, up or down, drawn as one picture everywhere: beside the Mover with its tail at the Mover and its tip toward the Spot, and on the Pick buttons. White with a dark edge, like Traffic Jam's.
_Avoid_: Direction, sign, command (a Robot Path word)

**Trip**:
One turn: the Treat shows, the Mover goes to it and comes back to the middle. In Pick a Trip may hold Wrong ways first.
_Avoid_: Round, go, turn, question

**Wrong way**:
In Pick, the Mover going where a wrong Arrow points: it reaches an empty Spot, shrugs and comes back. Then the right Arrow button glows; after a second Wrong way in a Trip the Arrow shows beside the Mover too. Nothing is counted.
_Avoid_: Mistake, miss, fail, wrong answer

**Cheer**:
Confetti and a big happy Mover after every fifth Trip, then the dots empty and Practice goes on.
_Avoid_: Reward, level up

### Practice

Which Way? is a Practice Game (the site's word): it has no Levels. Practice, Scope and Range are the site's words, in the site's CONTEXT.md.

**Way**:
Who makes the Mover go, picked by a grown-up on Practice. **Watch**: the Arrow shows and the Mover goes by itself. **Go**: the Arrow shows and the Mover waits for the Go button. **Pick**: no Arrow shows, and she taps the Arrow button that points at the Treat.
_Avoid_: Mode, step, level

**Go button**:
The big green button she taps in the Go Way to send the Mover along the Arrow. It sits where the Pick buttons do.
_Avoid_: Play button (Practice's), Next button

**Pick buttons**:
A row along the bottom in Pick, one per Arrow in the Scope, always in the order ← ↑ ↓ →, so she reads the Arrow rather than tapping the button nearest the Treat.
_Avoid_: Choices, options, answers

**Scope**:
Which Arrows come up: any of left, right, up and down, with the Ranges ←→, ↑↓ and All. Each comes up once before any comes again. A new save starts on Watch, ←→ and the puppy.
