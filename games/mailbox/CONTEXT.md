# Mailbox

A touch game that teaches three to five-year-olds which lowercase letter goes with each capital they know. The child posts lowercase letters into mailboxes that wear capitals, or finds the letter a mailbox asks for.

## Language

### Play

**Letter**:
One of the 26, a to z. Each has a capital and a lowercase form and one Name for both. In code a Letter is its lowercase form.
_Avoid_: Character, glyph (except about the font)

**Card**:
A lowercase letter on a white card, the Thing the child moves. It sits on a faint baseline, so p and q hang below it and b and d rise above it.
_Avoid_: Tile (My Letter's word), piece, block

**Mailbox**:
A blue mailbox on a post, side on, with a white plate wearing a capital. In Sort, two or three stand along the bottom, one for each Letter in the Round; in Find, one big Mailbox asks. They are all the same blue, so the capital is the only cue.
_Avoid_: Bin (the site's word, in code only), box, house (only the House button shows a house)

**Flag**:
The red flag on a Mailbox's side. It lies down until a Card goes in, then stands up: there's mail. At the end of a Round every flag is up.

**Bag**:
In Sort, the Round's Cards heaped small at the top, one fewer each time one hops out onto the Spot. Four for each of two Mailboxes, three for each of three, never three in a row for one Mailbox.
_Avoid_: Pile (the site's word, in code only), queue

**Spot**:
In Sort, where the next Card waits, above the middle, until it's dragged.

**Ask**:
In Find, the Mailbox turns its plate to a capital and says its Name; two or three Cards lie on the table, one of them the lowercase letter that goes with it. Six Asks to a Round. A tap on the Mailbox asks again.
_Avoid_: Question

**Name**:
A Letter's name, a recorded clip, the same word for its capital and its lowercase: "a", "bee". The Game says nothing else: never "capital", "lowercase", "big" or "little". In Sort a Mailbox's Name is said as its first Card goes in, after a Miss, when a Card is tapped, and from the second nudge; in Find only the Ask says one.
_Avoid_: Letter sound (My Letter's), Word, Voice

**Hint**:
On an Unlike or Tricky Letter's plate, its lowercase letter shown faintly beside the capital: "A a". It fades after a Round with no Miss on that Letter and is gone after two. A Miss brings it back. Same and Close Letters have none.
_Avoid_: Clue, ghost letter, Faint letters (My Letter's switch)

**Look-alikes**:
Two Letters a young child mixes up, as lowercase letters or as capitals: b d p q, g q, n u, m w, h n, n r, i j l, f t, a o, a d, c e, u v y, v w, and the capitals B D P R, C G O Q, E F, M N W, I J L T, K X. They share a Round only when the Scope leaves no other way.
_Avoid_: Confusables, similar

**Miss**:
In Sort, a Card given to the wrong Mailbox: a soft bump, it goes back, says its Name, and its own Mailbox glows. In Find, the wrong Card given: a soft bump, it goes back, and the Mailbox asks again. After the second Miss the right one goes in by itself. Nothing is counted, but a Miss keeps that Letter's Hint.
_Avoid_: Mistake, wrong answer

**Round**:
One Bag sorted, or six Asks answered, then every Flag up, the Mailboxes wobble, the site's cheer, a card for the grown-up ("Find an r in a book together!") and the Next button.
_Avoid_: Level, game

### Practice

Mailbox is a Practice Game (the site's word) with one Topic, Letters, so it shows no tabs. Practice, Scope and Range are the site's words.

**Scope**:
The Letters that come up, Aa to Zz on the buttons. Play waits for two. A new save has O and S.

**Ranges**:
The Letters by how much the lowercase looks like its capital, easiest first: **Same** (c o s v w x z, the same shape), **Close** (i j k p u y, one small change), **Unlike** (a e f h m n r t) and **Tricky** (b d g l q, which also look like each other), then All.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, group

**Way**:
📬 **Sort** (each lowercase Card into the Mailbox wearing its capital) or 🔍 **Find** (one Mailbox asks for its lowercase letter). A new save is on Sort.
_Avoid_: Mode
