# A straight track, and the Voice says the Square

The Track is one straight row from left to right, Start then 1 to Home, on a phone held either way, even though ten Squares in a row make them small in portrait. It's never bent into a snake, wrapped onto two rows or drawn round a circle.

Siegler & Ramani (2008) found that playing the race game on a straight 1–10 track improved preschoolers' number line estimates, magnitude comparison and counting, and that the same game on a circular board did not: the straight row is what maps "bigger" onto "further along". Siegler & Ramani (2009) also found that saying the number of each square landed on ("four, five") helped more than counting the hops ("one, two"), so the Voice says the Square's number on every Hop, the Friend's too.

To keep ten Squares readable in portrait, the child doesn't tap the Squares: they tap the big Spinner and the big Hopper button under the Track, and the animals stand in lanes above and below it.
