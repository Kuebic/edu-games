# Two players share one phone, and both get Home

A grown-up asked for a player 1 and player 2 option, so two children can race each other. With Two players, picked on the first screen, the Friend is the second child's: its turns wait for taps on the Spinner and the big animal button, like the Hopper's, and there's still one Spinner and one big button. The button shows whoever's turn it is, and the Voice says "Bunny's turn. Spin!" so the children know. Their animals keep their lanes, above and below the Track.

Nobody loses (ADR 0002), so a Race with Two players ends when both animals are Home: the first one there waits, the Voice says "Bunny is home! Keep hopping!", and the other takes every turn until it gets there too. Who's ahead? is asked as before, and either child may answer.

The second child plays the Race's Friend rather than picking an animal of their own. A second row of animal chips would crowd the first screen for a pick that matters less than who's playing, and a different Friend each Race keeps the Races looking different. We rejected two big buttons, one per child, as the Track's width leaves room under it for the Spinner and one button in portrait, and a button that's never the one to tap invites taps. We also rejected putting the switch in the Grown-up Corner: who's playing changes from one session to the next, so it's one tap on the first screen.
