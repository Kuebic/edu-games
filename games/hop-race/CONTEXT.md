# Hop Race

A touch game that teaches a three-year-old the numbers 1 to 10 in order: the child spins, hops their animal along a straight Track, and hears the number of every Square they land on.

## Language

### Play

**Track**:
The straight row of Squares, left to right: Start, then 1 to Home. Never bent or wrapped. Also the Game's word for a Group: To 5, To 10 and Who's ahead.
_Avoid_: Board, path, number line (in UI copy)

**Square**:
One numbered space on the Track, each a different colour.
_Avoid_: Space, cell, step

**Start**:
The plain mat before 1, where both animals begin.

**Home**:
The last Square, 5 or 10, marked with a flag. A Race is done when the Hopper gets there.
_Avoid_: Finish, goal, end

**Hopper**:
The child's own animal: Bunny, Puppy, Kitty or Bear, picked from the chips on the Track list. It's a Skin: it changes the picture and the name the Voice says, never the Races.
_Avoid_: Player, token, piece

**Friend**:
The other animal in a Race, a different one each Race of a Track. With One player it spins and hops by itself, and never wins. With Two players a second child taps its turns.
_Avoid_: Rival, opponent, computer

**Players**:
One or Two, picked from the chips under the Tracks. One is the child against a Friend that moves itself. Two is two children taking turns on one phone, the second playing the Friend: the Voice says whose turn it is, the big button shows that animal, and the Race goes on until both are Home (ADR 0003). Saved in the Game's slot, and a reset keeps it.
_Avoid_: Mode, multiplayer, versus

**Spinner**:
The round button that says how far to hop: the left half with one dot is 1, the right half with two is 2.
_Avoid_: Die, dice, wheel

**Turn**:
One spin and its Hops, the Hopper's or the Friend's. They alternate, the Hopper first; an animal at Home is skipped.

**Hop**:
One move onto the next Square. The Voice says the Square's number. On a tapped turn, each Hop is a tap on the big animal button.
_Avoid_: Step, move, jump

**Who's ahead?**:
The question between turns in the third Track: the Hopper and the Friend as two big buttons, and the child taps the one further along. A wrong pick Fades, as in My Letter.
_Avoid_: Quiz, compare

**Nudge**:
After 6 seconds with no tap on the child's turn, the glowing button wiggles and the Voice asks again.

### Progress

**Race**:
The Game's Level: from Start until the Hopper is Home (with Two players, both animals), then the cheer and the Next button.
_Avoid_: Game, round, match
