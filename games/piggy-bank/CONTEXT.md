# Piggy Bank

A touch game that teaches two to four-year-olds the names of US money. The child puts coins and a dollar bill into piggy banks, by their pictures or by their names.

## Language

### Play

**Money**:
The five kinds the Game knows: penny, nickel, dime, quarter and dollar (the $1 bill). Each is a real photo, heads and tails, and its name is its Word. The Game says names only, never values.
_Avoid_: Coin (the dollar is a bill), currency, denomination

**Piece**:
One penny, nickel, dime, quarter or dollar bill on the screen, drawn at its true size: every coin is its real diameter times one scale, so the dime is the smallest and the quarter the biggest. The bill is not to scale.
_Avoid_: Coin (in code, where a bill is a Piece too), Thing (Tidy Up's word), token

**Heads** and **Tails**:
A Piece's two sides. It comes out heads up. In Sort a tap turns it over, and back, and says its Word. Tails never decides where a Piece goes.
_Avoid_: Front/back, obverse/reverse (in UI copy)

**Pig**:
A see-through pink piggy bank. What goes in through its slot lies in its belly, so it fills up.
_Avoid_: Bin (Tidy Up's word), jar, bank (alone)

**Bank**:
In Sort, a Pig wearing one kind of Money's heads photo on a tag, at true relative size. Two or three stand along the bottom, as many as the Scope has kinds, up to three. The Pigs are all one pink, so the tag is the only cue.
_Avoid_: Bin, target

**Purse**:
In Sort, the Round's Pieces heaped small at the top, one fewer each time one hops out onto the Spot. Four for each of two Banks, three for each of three, never three in a row for one Bank.
_Avoid_: Pile (Tidy Up's word), queue, wallet

**Spot**:
In Sort, where the next Piece waits, above the middle, until it's dragged.

**Ask**:
In Find, the Pig's bubble pops up and its Word says what it wants: one of the two or three Pieces on the table, one of each kind. Six Asks to a Round, spread over the kinds; the Pieces change places between them. A tap on the Pig asks again. With no sound, the bubble shows the Piece's picture instead.
_Avoid_: Question, Find (Find It's word)

**Word**:
The recorded name of a kind of Money. In Sort it's said as a Piece goes in, after a Miss and when a Piece is turned over. In Find only the Ask says one, so a Word there always names the Piece asked for. The Game has no Voice.
_Avoid_: Voice, label

**Miss**:
In Sort, a Piece given to the wrong Pig: a soft bump, it goes back and says its own Word, and its Bank glows. In Find, the wrong Piece given: a soft bump, it goes back, and the Pig asks again. After the second Miss, the right one glows and goes in by itself. Nothing is counted.
_Avoid_: Mistake, wrong answer

**Nudge**:
After 7 seconds without a touch. Sort: the Piece wiggles and its Bank glows, with its Word from the second. Find: the bubble wiggles, the Ask is said again from the second, and the Piece asked for glows from the third. Three at most in a row.
_Avoid_: Hint, timeout

**Round**:
One Purse sorted, or six Asks answered, then the Pigs jingle and wobble, the site's cheer, a card for the grown-up ("Find a penny with a grown-up!") and the Next button.
_Avoid_: Level, game

### Practice

Piggy Bank is a Practice Game (the site's word) with one Topic, Money, so it shows no tabs. Practice, Scope and Range are the site's words.

**Scope**:
The kinds that come up: 1¢, 5¢, 10¢, 25¢, $1 on the buttons, which only a grown-up reads. Play waits for two. The Ranges are 1¢ 25¢, 1¢ 10¢ 25¢, and All. A new save has the penny and the quarter.

**Way**:
🐷 **Sort** (each Piece into the Bank with its picture) or 🔍 **Find** (the Pig asks for one by name). A new save is on Sort.
_Avoid_: Mode, level
