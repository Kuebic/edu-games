# No scores, timers, or failure states

The game has no points, star ratings, timers, lives, or "wrong" buzzers, and every finished Round earns a Sticker no matter how many picks were wrong. A wrong pick starts a Count-Along instead of a penalty. The player is a pre-reader who is still learning what adding means, and the guidance we found on preschool touch apps says to remove competition and time pressure so attention stays on the Act-Out. First Try results are tracked only to move Stages, never shown to the child.
